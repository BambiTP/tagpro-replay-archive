What this is

A record of every ranked TagPro match the replay listing returns: 123,909 matches since Mar 2025, each with its id, start time, map and duration, and a tagpro.eu record for 121,636 of them. For 10,530 the recording itself is held too.

It exists out of a love for data.

Why there are so few recordings

Scraping replays older than two days is forbidden, and the site blocks viewing a replay older than two days without an account. A request for a bulk download of the older material was declined.

That is the whole reason the two halves of this site look so different. Ids can be listed in bulk, so every match has one. A recording has to be fetched inside the two-day window, so the archive holds them only from the point collection started, and only where it kept up.

Bulk replay dumps wanted

If you have replay dumps — exports, scrapes, anything — send them to metjr_ on Discord. Any format, any size, any dates.

Recordings are keyed by uuid, so duplicates cost nothing and nothing needs checking or filtering first. The list of what is missing is published if it is useful.

Ask for what you need. Message metjr_ on Discord for a particular cut of the data, a different format, a query run across the whole archive, or help with a project you are building on it. Requests are welcome and are usually quicker to answer than they are to work around.

Where the numbers come from

  • The ranked replay listing decides what exists. Every id here came from it, so the totals are counts, not estimates.
  • tagpro.eu is the second source, and where the box score, events and players come from. About 98% of matches have one. The two are matched on start time with 120 seconds of slack, because they record it in different time zones and to different precision.
  • 292 tagpro.eu records were rebuilt here from recordings, for matches the mirror never had. That is the orange band on the coverage table, and there is a page on how accurate they are.
  • 8 matches are exceptions. They are on tagpro.eu but the listing returns nothing for them, even inside ranges it says are complete. There is no recording to get.

Two ids identify a match: uuid for the replay, game_id for the recording (that is the one you request a recording by), and eu_match_id for the same match on tagpro.eu. Full field list in DATA_MAP.md.

How it runs

A pipeline follows the listing, pulls each replay while it is still reachable, links it to its tagpro.eu record, and rebuilds records for matches the mirror missed. This site is regenerated from that database on a schedule. The 3.9 GB of recordings stay on the machine that collected them and are served from it — see Download.

Numbers on this page come from the same database as the coverage tables and move with them. Field reference: DATA_MAP.md.
