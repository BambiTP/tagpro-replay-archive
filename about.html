What this is

A record of every ranked TagPro match I can get at: 123,909 matches since Mar 2025, each with its id, start time, map and duration, and a tagpro.eu record for 121,636 of them. For 10,530 I also have the recording itself.

I wanted this data and nobody had it. Ranked games happen, get listed for a couple of days, and then you can't query them any more. So I started saving them.

Why there are so few recordings

I can't get the old ones. Scraping replays older than two days isn't allowed, and the site won't show you a replay older than two days without an account. I asked for a bulk download of everything already past that point and was turned down.

So I take them as they happen. The ids are easy: the listing hands over whole date ranges at once, which is why that side is complete. The recordings are one file per match, fetched while the match is still inside the window, which is why that side is not. Everything older than two days when I started was already out of reach.

Every week from here on gets collected in full. The old matches aren't coming back on their own.

Send me your replays

If you have replay dumps sitting around — exports, scrapes, a folder from two years ago, anything — send them to metjr_ on Discord. Any format, any size, any dates, however messy.

I match on uuid, so duplicates cost nothing and you don't need to check what I already have or filter anything out first. A dump where 90% is already here is still 10% I didn't have. There is a list of what is missing if you want to aim at the gaps, but don't let that stop you sending the whole thing.

Where the numbers come from

  • The ranked replay listing decides what exists. Every id on this site came from it, so the totals are counts, not estimates.
  • tagpro.eu is the second source, and where the box score, events and players come from. About 98% of matches have one. The two are matched on start time with 120 seconds of slack, because they record it in different time zones and to different precision.
  • 292 tagpro.eu records were rebuilt here from recordings, for matches the mirror never had. That is the orange band on the coverage table.
  • 8 matches are exceptions. They are on tagpro.eu but the listing returns nothing for them, even inside ranges it says are complete. There's no recording to get.

Two ids identify a match: uuid for the replay, game_id for the recording (that is the one you request a recording by), and eu_match_id for the same match on tagpro.eu. Full field list in DATA_MAP.md.

How it runs

A pipeline on a machine at my house follows the listing, pulls each replay while it is still there, links it to its tagpro.eu record, and rebuilds records for matches the mirror missed. This site is regenerated from that database on a schedule. The 3.9 GB of recordings stay on that machine and are served from it — see Download.

Numbers on this page come from the same database as the coverage tables and move with them. Field reference: DATA_MAP.md.
